About Sonic Auto

Who we are

Sonic Auto is a Richards Bay-based automotive parts supplier built for the trade. We supply workshops, panel beaters, and fleet operators across northern KwaZulu-Natal with quality parts from brands that professionals know and trust.

What we stand for

Our model is simple: quality only. No imitation parts. No shortcuts. Every part we supply is sourced from reputable manufacturers — because a comeback costs your customer time and costs you trust. When a part leaves our shelf, it should go in and stay in.

That's why our slogan is what it is: Solid Parts. Fit and Forget.

Our brands

We stock and supply from a carefully selected range of OE-quality brands including GMB, KOYO, GSP, and Mahle — names that need no introduction in any workshop worth its bay.

Where we are headed

We are currently serving the Richards Bay and northern KZN trade market, with plans to extend our reach into Gauteng. As we grow, our commitment stays the same — to be the supplier that trade professionals can rely on, every time.
